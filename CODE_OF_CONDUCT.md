# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in TerraWatch welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussion and review.
- Accept feedback graciously and focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Share relevant context and help keep collaboration clear and inclusive.
- Respect the privacy and safety of other contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer through the repository hosting platform. Do not post reports publicly.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces.
